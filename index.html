WELCOME TO ORANGE SKY

A quiet hideaway where the hills do the talking

Orange Sky is a boutique retreat in Purakkadi, set against the layered green of the Wayanad hills. We keep things intentional — a handful of rooms instead of a hundred, home-cooked Kerala meals instead of buffets, and evenings that end around a fire under an orange sky.

Whether you are a family slowing down for a weekend, a couple looking for a quiet corner with a pool, or a group of travellers exploring Wayanad together, the retreat is designed to feel personal, warm, and unhurried.
